**What each contract card makes untrue.** WL-4 and WL-6 change the order "collect, reset, then return": tests in `tests/packs/runpod/test_machine.py` and `test_job_on_claimed_machine.py` that pin the operation sequence of a second job must be updated to the new budget, not deleted. WL-8 replaces polling: tests that drive completion by advancing a poll must drive the event instead. WL-9 changes how many times delivery verifies: tests asserting a re-verification after copy must assert the single digest is checked against the published object.
