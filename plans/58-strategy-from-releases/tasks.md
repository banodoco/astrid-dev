## SR-1: what it makes untrue

- `check_docs.py:release_errors`: "Included plans must link project plan.md files" stops applying to a draft.
- `test_check_docs.py`: the `RELEASE` fixture gains `Toward`; `test_release_requires_sections_and_plans` asserts the old message and is rewritten for the final-brief rule.
- `scripts/fixtures/golden.txt`: the `release-no-plan-links` line for the old message goes; the folders `final-release-without-payload`, `release-included-plans-unlinked`, `release-missing-sections-and-plans` and `release-no-plan-links` replace `releases/12.md` with older copies that have no `Toward`, so each either gains the line or gains one new message. Choose per folder and say which.
- `records.named_field_matches` reads the next line for an empty value; use `key_value_lines` or a stricter pattern.

## SR-4: what it makes untrue

- `check_docs.py`: `STRATEGY_HEADER`, `RELEASE_ROW`, `STRATEGY_PLAN`, the row loop in `strategy_errors`, and in `version_errors` the `Plans` and `Releases` rules. `STRATEGY_SECTIONS` loses `## Sequence`.
- `test_check_docs.py`, `StrategyTests`: the `GOOD` fixture; `test_release_requires_matching_roadmap_row` and `test_summary_and_plans_must_match_document` are removed (a generated part cannot disagree with its source; the view's own test covers a hand edit); the three version tests are rewritten for sentences and `Toward`; `test_bad_release_link_and_missing_section_fail` keeps its missing-section half.
- `scripts/fixtures/`: `strategy-highlight-mismatch`, `strategy-missing-release-document`, `strategy-plan-links-mismatch` and `strategy-release-mismatch` pin failures that can no longer happen and are removed with their `golden.txt` lines; `strategy-missing-section-and-bad-release-link` keeps its missing-section case. The `golden.txt` lines for the four release folders that mention the strategy row go.
- `check_completion.py:strategy_errors`, its call in `checks.py:_completion_impl`, and `test_check_completion.py` (the cases that require `strategy.md` among the changed paths) are removed.
- The registry description of `strategy-consistency` changes, so `docs/checks.md` changes by `sync`.
