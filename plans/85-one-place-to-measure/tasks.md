[Plan](plan.md) · [Inputs](inputs.md)

**Task ids:** `MS-1` to `MS-5`, `R`. **Critical path:** MS-1, MS-3, MS-4, R. **Test inventory:** `python3 scripts/astrid_dev.py check` and the unit tests under `scripts/`; nothing in a product repository is changed by this plan. Paths were read on astrid-dev `main` on 22 November 2026 and are re-checked at dispatch.

Every card: no product behaviour changes; evidence already recorded stays where it is, and a script that moves leaves a pointer; a skill changes through the [improve workstream](../../skills/shared/workstreams/astrid-dev-improve/SKILL.md), starting from a recorded failure. Nothing generated and no new check is built in this plan.
