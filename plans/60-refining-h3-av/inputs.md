## Owner decisions

- **D1 — Remove LanPaint.** Options: remove the helper, the packaged workflow and its tests; or keep it as a dormant candidate. Recommend remove. Cost stated plainly: it was the only graph that could regenerate sound inside existing footage, it never ran on a GPU, and nothing replaces that; new dialogue goes through continuation.
- **D2 — One request shape.** Options: (a) document version 1 as the one shape, keep version 2 only as far as the character swap needs it and strip its unimplemented parts; (b) delete version 2 and move the character swap into version 1; (c) keep both documented. Recommend (a), with (b) filed as an issue if the character swap proves to be used.
- **D3 — Remove the `astrid h3` command.** Options: remove it so `astrid generate video --entry h3` is the one way in; or keep it and fix its hard-coded local placement. Recommend remove; its planning use is covered by `--dry-run`.
- **D4 — Footage that is not 16:9.** When H3 prepares a source whose shape differs from 1024×576, options: fill the frame and crop the excess, or fit inside with bars. Recommend fill as the default, always stated in the request's dry-run output and overridable per request, and never a stretch. Fill matches the no-black-bars direction for the Matrix shots.
- **D5 — Where transcription and masks live.** Recommend as written under Approach: both as general default workflows fixed under Plan 59 when its tasks reach them; the single transcript structure as its own later plan; none of them inside the H3_AV pack.
- **D6 — Release and review.** Recommend including this plan in Release 14 beside Plan 59, on `release/14/plan-60` branches, with one review round at the end.

Open until the GPU pilot, not yet a decision: whether 1.6 seconds of voice context is enough. If not, the options are a longer context window (to be tested on the graph) or accepting drift on long lines.
