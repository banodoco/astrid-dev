### Alternatives

- Clone Dev's full pipeline and roles: imposes release membership, three-file packs and binary completion where creative/continuous work differs; reject.
- Operate with a free-form folder of copy only: fast for one draft, but loses campaign/evidence/learning links and permission context. Appropriate for tiny work inside the workspace, not the organisation's whole system.
- Design a comprehensive marketing machine and automate every channel now: premature; unknown audiences, integrations, attribution and publication permissions. Defer until evidence makes a capability worth building.
- Use Plan 95's minimal consumer and add just the local records, skills, views and policies the first real work needs: recommended. Reuse its pilot where valid, keep raw analytics and private contacts in authoritative systems, and use manual authorized publication/measurement initially.
- Start marketing discovery while the framework is unfinished: optional read-only owner-authorized activity, no duplicate framework or premature live operation; adopt the accepted handoff before implementation.

### Evidence distinctions

A completed asset is not a published asset; a published asset is not a measured response; measured response is not incremental impact. Every report names what happened, where it was observed, denominator and window, material missing data and competing explanations. An experiment with negative or inconclusive findings can complete if the method and observation were sound. Evidence can be qualitative where appropriate; no fake precision or need for an engineering test receipt for a taste judgment.
