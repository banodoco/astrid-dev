### What changed from the earlier proposal

The initial Plan 96 in [PR #1868](https://github.com/banodoco/astrid-dev/pull/1868) treated audiences, positioning, objectives, experiments, content and results as a more elaborate graph and required a live distribution/measurement cycle for completion. That is too much for initial setup. These can be documents within projects or knowledge, with optional new kinds only after actual demand. Campaigns group projects; issues are already a natural idea intake. One project may itself be a small campaign. No automatic publishing or recurring scheduler.

Alternatives: clone Dev (wrong release/number/approval rules); elaborate marketing pipeline or platform (premature); unstructured folder of drafts (fine for a task but loses durable project/idea context); minimal Otto workspace with reusable templates and local campaigns (recommended). Distinguish actual produced/reviewed content from distribution, observed response and attributable impact. Record only what the evidence supports. Use public-safe examples and exact verified product facts, not private source copies.
