### Sequencing and constraints

A–E can start as read-only investigations in parallel; do not assume an independent implementation writer for shared launcher/policy files. Agree one owner and landing order for shared policy/service catalog, guest mode/instance contract and onboarding choices. The security/compatibility design review precedes implementation of its dependent surfaces; F requires the integrated candidate, qualified platform and approved proof route. A mount-free attached ComfyUI proof need not wait for a separate GPU worker or universal custom-node compatibility. An agent unavailable in the VM must say so; it may not fall back to native.

Keep native and strict modes separate. The connected profile is an explicitly accepted host service exception, not equivalent to the strict profile. Avoid broad refactors just to increase parallelism. Scope overlapping active work with [onboarding](../20-zero-to-first-image/plan.md), [agent setup](../16-streamlined-agent-experience/plan.md), [generation placement](../32-generation-placement-targets/plan.md) and [inference routing](../12-multiple-inference-routes/plan.md) at their actual shared contracts.
