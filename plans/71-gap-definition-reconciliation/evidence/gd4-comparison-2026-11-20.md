## What the comparison establishes — and what it does not

**Preliminary alignment:** The two passes make the same immediate *no-unreviewed-gap-creation* decision in all 16 cases, but that coarse agreement hides **four unresolved boundary families** (editing durability/legacy revision, artistic reference control, reusable spatial regions and user-visible local responsiveness). Evidence strengths and alternative neighbors differ in CAL-10, CAL-15 and CAL-16. A percentage agreement would be misleading without these distinctions.

**Potential rubric weaknesses for RS1 to challenge:**
1. “Materially unproven” is too easy to mistake for “absent.” Require a named discriminating test and no new gap if only a specific verification task is missing.
2. Two outcomes under one primary vision goal can be distinct (F-005/F-006); independent closure is the deciding criterion, not shared providers/goal text.
3. Multiple bugs with the same symptom may still have different causes, but a stable recipient outcome can be one gap. The rubric needs to articulate when evidence clusters share a closure case instead of counting issues.
4. The present rubric does not numerically define “meaningful,” which is acceptable only if the reviewer can justify the recipient and a falsifiable outcome against nearest gaps—not merely name one.
5. Creative control may be a small but independently closable result even if a broad “sustained artwork” goal is not closed; explicitly test that ambiguity before creating or dismissing a gap.

**No holdout inspection:** The ten HOLD sources are still sealed. Before testing them, a separate high-judgment reviewer should judge the frozen rubric and whether these disagreements invalidate the chosen abstraction; record genuine failure and version changes before accessing them.

**Next review contract:** A qualified independent reviewer must read source-linked CAL evidence and both case assessments, challenge every unresolved family with concrete positive/negative proof, identify any failed rubric question, and either accept a revised freeze or return specific correction rows. **RS1 is separate** and must be followed by explicit owner GD-5 acceptance before GD-6; the author of this comparison does not claim either.
