## 2. Recommendation and decision rule

R16 should make the ordinary Pinokio menu truthful from installation through a creative result and safe update. Reuse the shipped launcher, C2 installer/Runtime authority, explicit controller, staged editor, process identity and existing proof tools. Do not build another lifecycle service, wrapper simulating Pinokio, or second updater. The major unresolved prerequisite is outside ordinary descriptor code: can the supported *actual* engine settle or veto every native Stop/Quit operation before its shell tree is killed? If not, release remains unqualified; stop at the missing upstream API and obtain a supported upstream build/contract or an explicitly authorized compatible minimal upstream change. A user-visible Stop menu that works while Pinokio's own native stop/quit loses work is not dependable. Do not silently delete native support or label the old fake kernel as native acceptance.

The release proof must distinguish two axes: Astrid managed by Pinokio (hosted editor with owned local Runtime, or fully local editor + Runtime) and an externally owned ComfyUI launched by Pinokio (possibly on a direct Astrid install). Those are not interchangeable ownership or environment-discovery rows. The latter's existing server is discovered/reused with permission, not managed by the Astrid launcher. No terminating another app's ComfyUI, foreign Runtime, service, queued job, or deleting models/data merely because it was discovered.
