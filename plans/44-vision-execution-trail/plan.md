## Goal and scope

Make the recently revamped astrid-dev and Astrid vision page reveal a maintained chain from stated ambitions through gaps, projects, experiments, evidence, decisions and real outcomes.

**Included:** Traceable project evidence, decisions, outcomes and gap reassessments presented from canonical source without parallel editorial metadata.

**Boundary:** No duplicate tracker and no automatic gap closure when a project completes.

## What this builds on

[Vision index](../../vision/README.md), [project catalog](../index.md) and [public website plan](../21-astrid-website/plan.md). These are foundations to verify at current source revisions, not proof of a deployed result. Prefer reusing them over inventing another runtime or state owner.

## Proposed sequence

1. Audit existing experiment and decision evidence and identify missing connections.
2. Connect each project to a single primary gap and to specific outputs, tests and decisions.
3. Expose a readable versioned evidence trail on the public vision page.
4. Have unfamiliar contributors trace three decisions including a failed or changed approach and resolve observed confusion.

## Interfaces and handoff

Before product work, pin actual accepted base/donor revisions, implementation owners and dependencies. Consume existing pack/component/project contracts; produce a tested contract or working artifact and link exact product PRs. Never infer authorization from a draft.

## Completion

Have unfamiliar contributors trace three decisions including a failed or changed approach and resolve observed confusion. Record integrated candidate identity, real creator/agent outcomes and meaningful negative cases. Completion does not automatically close the associated vision gap.

**Authorization:** Draft, unassigned, queued. Owner decision and focused checks are required before implementation.
