## Stage 2: 200 ms (conditional)

**Started only on stage 1's numbers: if FS-1 and the work above reach under a second and the owner does not feel the rest, stage 2 does not start.** 200 ms at the median and 500 ms at the 95th percentile is worth having only if it comes from the principled change below, not from tuning. **Not on the list:** a faster language, a second process, an "accept now, validate later" reply (a refusal must stay an answer to the request), or shrinking what SQLite stores (all of it is under 0.1 s, measured).
