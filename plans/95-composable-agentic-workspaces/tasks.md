**Lanes.** *Orchestrator* cards need clones, network, repository or branch administration, or a review verdict, and run in the orchestrator's session. *Packet* cards write code, tests or documents in one fresh ChatGPT session, in the mode the route sets (`N` High, `X` Extra High, `S` Pro; `T` per the run's brief). Packet sessions do not run tests: the orchestrator runs each packet's focused proof on `READY-PROOF` ([dispatch](../../agent/roles/orchestrator/multi-manager-dispatch.md)). Each packet card is sized at about three hours or less.

**Branches (proposed, settled with D3).** Otto: `p95/<card id in lower case>` into Otto `main`; Otto has no consumer until OT-23 tags it. astrid-dev: `project/95-otto` from `main`, with `p95/<card>` branches into it; progress reaches `main` only as `tasks.md`-only progress PRs, and the project branch reaches `main` only through OT-24 with the owner's go.

### Dispatch boundaries

Do not start before Plans 71–73 are on `main`, and do not change their branches or tasks. No card touches a product repository, a release brief or `releases/`. Otto holds no secrets, and its CI needs none. A packet that would change a Dev test assertion, fixture or oracle line not listed in its card stops and reports. Never run `skills install` without `--destination` pointing at a temporary directory.
