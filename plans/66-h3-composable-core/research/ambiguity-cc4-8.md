## CC-4 — Guides: soft image, clip or audio at a frame

### Resolved from code

- A guide is a timeline occurrence with `role: guide`; it is not a `reference` and does not create an exact owner or edit grant. Keep `timeline` semantics for exact material. Do not reuse `hard: false`: existing exact placements default to that value. Use the guide item’s own typed modality and `at` position. `MiniMaxH3AddGuide` accepts conditioning, latent, optional video VAE, optional audio VAE, optional image, optional audio and integer `frame_idx` ([official node](https://github.com/Comfy-Org/ComfyUI/blob/6bfaacc/comfy_extras/nodes_minimax_h3.py#L165-L190)); the v2 normalizer already distinguishes `timeline`, `reference`, timing and media occurrences (`shared/request_v2.py:127-161`, `350-423`).
- `at.frame` is a zero-based pixel-frame index at 24 fps. `at.seconds` must convert to that frame grid. Resolve `-1` in Astrid to `delivered_frame_count - 1` and pass that nonnegative index to the node. The stock node resolves negative values against the sampled latent frame count, which may include trimmed H3 tail frames, so forwarding `-1` can guide a frame that is not delivered. It computes `frame_count` from latent video token spans, then resolves `frame_count + frame_idx` ([official node](https://github.com/Comfy-Org/ComfyUI/blob/6bfaacc/comfy_extras/nodes_minimax_h3.py#L192-L217)); H3 output length rounds to a latent run and Astrid separately tracks trimming (`shared/generation.py:195-202`).
- An image guide needs the video VAE; an audio guide needs the audio VAE; a guide may carry both. Its image batch is a clip: 1–4 input frames use only the first; at 5 or more, crop the batch down to the largest `17k+5` length (5, 22, 39, …). Refuse if the retained clip does not fit starting at its frame. The node’s exact behavior is in its `guide_frames` and fit checks ([official node](https://github.com/Comfy-Org/ComfyUI/blob/6bfaacc/comfy_extras/nodes_minimax_h3.py#L201-L217)). Thus replace “2–4 frames contribute one image” with “1–4 frames contribute the first image.”
- Audio is encoded by the audio VAE and cropped on the H3 40 Hz latent clock to the remaining audio steps. When its start is beyond that clock, the node refuses. It reports neither exact delivered ownership nor an audio sample splice; it adds conditioning only ([official node](https://github.com/Comfy-Org/ComfyUI/blob/6bfaacc/comfy_extras/nodes_minimax_h3.py#L224-L235)). Dry-run should show original occurrence, requested position, resolved delivered frame, retained video-frame count (if any), and audio tick crop separately.
- Chain one `MiniMaxH3AddGuide` per guide in a stable request occurrence order. Each node consumes the previous conditioning output and the same target latent; this accumulates `minimax_keyframes` rather than creating multiple owners ([official node](https://github.com/Comfy-Org/ComfyUI/blob/6bfaacc/comfy_extras/nodes_minimax_h3.py#L236-L239)). A guide may share its frame with an exact placement because it is conditioning, not ownership. Two exact placements claiming the same stream/frame still need the existing overlap refusal.
- Guides affect positive conditioning; they do not pin pixels or samples in the decoded output. Composition remains responsible for exact delivery. Do not call guide adherence “exact.”

### Exact replacement text for the card

> Add `role: guide` for a soft image, video or audio occurrence at a zero-based 24 fps frame. Keep `role: timeline` exact and keep `edit.guides` as reference-occurrence links. Resolve `at: -1` to the last delivered frame in preparation and bind that positive frame index; never pass `-1` through to the node. Chain one `MiniMaxH3AddGuide` per guide in normalized occurrence order, reusing the target latent and feeding each output conditioning to the next. Connect video VAE for image/video and audio VAE for audio. For an image batch, 1–4 frames use the first frame; 5 or more frames are cropped down to the largest length 5, 22, 39, …; refuse when that retained clip runs past the target. Crop audio conditioning to the remaining 40 Hz latent ticks and refuse a start beyond the audio track. Record requested position, resolved frame, retained image-frame count and audio crop in dry-run. Guides are soft conditioning only; exact delivered pixels or samples come from composition. A guide may coexist with an exact placement; overlapping exact owners remain refused.

### Misread/stall/build-wrong risks closed

The present card leaves unclear whether `-1` is passed through; whether video clips use requested or cropped length for fit; whether 1-frame image guides are legal; which VAE is required; whether audio means exact audio replacement; whether ordering is stable; whether guide placement grants write permission; and whether dry-run reports source or delivered coordinates. The quoted replacement settles each from the node contract and existing request lifecycle.

## CC-8 — Exact stills at any frame, per-item composition

### Resolved from code

- The official H3 video latent uses `FRAME_PER_TOKEN = (1, 4, 4, 4, 4)` and `FRAME_RESCALE = 5/3` ([`comfy/ldm/minimax/model.py`](https://github.com/Comfy-Org/ComfyUI/blob/6bfaacc/comfy/ldm/minimax/model.py#L30-L31)). The cumulative frame chunks are `[0,1)`, `[1,5)`, `[5,9)`, `[9,13)`, `[13,17)`, then the pattern repeats: frame 0, 17, 34, 51, … each begins a one-frame chunk; other frames lie in a four-frame chunk. This confirms the card’s claim. For example frame 61 is in `[60,64)` and holds four frames under `hold: chunk`.
- V2V spatial masks are resized spatially to latent H/W and collapsed over each temporal chunk. `mask_temporal_reduce="max"` takes the maximum value among that chunk’s frames ([motion pack `h3_v2v_fractional.py:218-250`](https://github.com/seitanism/ComfyUI-H3-Motion-Context-MultiRef/blob/361624fb/h3_v2v_fractional.py#L218-L250)). In the current Astrid graph, white means editable; `inside_strength` is applied where mask is white and `outside_strength` elsewhere (`shared/generation.py:377-418`; motion pack `h3_v2v_fractional.py:412-452`). Therefore a one-frame white edit in a four-frame chunk makes the whole latent chunk editable under `max`; an exact still must set the whole chunk to preserve/noise-mask 0. For partial-frame `hold: frame`, generated latent influence applies to the whole chunk and composition restores the other pixels after decode, which is why the card correctly warns of a possible discontinuity.
- Preparation needs a deterministic full-canvas source timeline long enough for the requested output, initialized from exact placed stills and any declared source media; gaps can be stand-in pixels because composition owns the final generated pixels there. Keep source frame count, requested/delivered frame count and H3 rounded frame count distinct. H3 rounds runs to `17k+5` (5, 22, …), so latent mask padding must not accidentally pin a rounded tail that is not delivered (`shared/generation.py:195-202`, masked source tail handling `:364-393`).
- Composition must key every placement by occurrence and use that occurrence’s source offset and destination span. The current `_compose_media` makes source-vs-generated decisions from interval lists and one `source_offset`; it reads one source and one generated candidate (`shared/compose.py:820-850,889-909`). CC-8 therefore changes that boundary: route each segment to its occurrence source (or generated candidate), with its own source offset, rather than assuming one authoritative source. A still’s hold span is destination ownership; its one-frame source is repeated for that span. Generated frames fill uncovered spans. Refuse overlapping exact owners on the same stream, independent of whether they came from the same asset. Fixed audio and video placements keep their own stream policies; an image still must not become an audio owner.
- “Stills at both 0 and the end with generated time between” is only unambiguous when output duration and hold policy make the spans non-overlapping. “At the last delivered frame” means the final delivered index, not the H3 padded latent’s last frame. `hold: chunk` on a four-frame chunk containing the final delivered frame may extend beyond output; clip its delivered span at output end while keeping its complete latent chunk masked.
- The `hold: frame`/`hold: chunk` choice is already stated in the task, so no extra approval is needed. Exact pixels are guaranteed by the composer, not VAE or latent masking.

### Exact replacement text for the card

> Add exact still timeline items with `hold: chunk` by default and optional `hold: frame`. H3 video chunks have lengths 1,4,4,4,4 repeating over frames, so chunks start at 0,1,5,9,13,17, …; frames 0,17,34,51, … are one-frame chunks. For `hold: chunk`, snap the requested frame to its containing chunk and record the clipped delivered span; at output end, clip delivery to the last requested frame but still freeze the full corresponding latent chunk. For `hold: frame`, freeze that H3 chunk during sampling only as required by the single latent mask, then compose the still pixel on the requested frame alone; other frames in the chunk remain generated and the dry-run warns of a possible visual jump. Use one source occurrence per placed item, keyed by occurrence, with its own destination span and source offset. Repeat still pixels over its delivered span; generate uncovered gaps; preserve each item’s stream-specific ownership. Refuse any overlapping exact owners for the same stream. Build a full-canvas preparation source and mask at target dimensions; do not treat padding frames as delivered frames. Keep requested, latent-rounded and delivered counts distinct. Dry-run lists requested frame, snapped latent chunk, delivered hold, occurrence and source offset.

### Misread/stall/build-wrong risks closed

The current detail does not state chunk interval endpoints, what to do when a chunk straddles output end, how a one-frame still becomes a multi-frame source, what source to use in gaps, whether `hold: frame` masks just one latent frame (impossible), how occurrence identity affects composition, how mixed modality ownership works, or which frame count defines “last.” The replacement supplies those mechanics. The “last delivered frame” and `hold: frame` test should use a request whose H3 rounded count exceeds delivered count, so the wrong indexing is observable.

## CC-5 — Templates: list, show, fill, write, submit

There is no template engine in the inspected H3 pack snapshot; this card defines a new CLI and data format. The following replacement fixes the contract so the author does not invent subtly incompatible variants. Plan 59 supplies shot/project/timeline/occurrence identities; its shot plan has multiple occurrences and the SDK validates declared fields (`astrid/sdk/shot_plan.py:418-436,474-489`, as reviewed in Plan 66 `research/critique.md`).

Use one JSON document with `template` metadata and a `request` object (the latter is the v2 request). For example: `{"template":{"name":"dialogue","purpose":"...","holes":[{"name":"line","type":"string","help":"...","source":{"set":"line"}}]},"request":{"version":2,"prompt":{"text":{"$hole":"line"}}}}`. `--write` serializes only the filled `request` object. This resolves whether the holes header is a comment/front matter or request field without changing the submitted v2 schema.

### Exact replacement text for the card

> Store pack recipes as installed UTF-8 JSON files under `astrid/packs/h3_av/docs/recipes/`; also discover each installed skill’s `recipes/` directory as required by the CLI contract. Each file is one ordinary v2 request plus a `holes` header. A hole has a unique name, JSON type (`string`, `number`, `integer`, `boolean`, `object`, or `array`), optional units, help, and exactly one source (`--set NAME=JSON` or a named shot field/occurrence). Use a dedicated JSON marker object `{"$hole":"name"}` as a whole JSON value; do not interpolate inside strings. Parse `--set` values as JSON and reject a type mismatch, unknown hole, duplicate fill, missing fill, unknown marker, or leftover marker with the hole name and its declared source. No defaults, expressions, conditionals, coercion or implicit string formatting; a variant is another recipe. For shot fills, require project, timeline and shot; select the exact occurrence when a hole maps to media whose trim or placement depends on occurrence; resolve its asset through the ordinary asset-map path. It does not update shot takes. Test every installed recipe using typed fixture values, including a quoted dialogue string, list-valued references, numeric duration, reused shot with explicit occurrence, missing value refusal, dry-run and compilation; submit a written request through the normal request path.

## Plan 64 SR-5 — Resolve the audio-mask unknown from motion pack `361624fb`

### Node contract and mask conversion

- `MiniMaxH3SetAVNoiseMask.INPUT_TYPES` requires `latent: LATENT`; optional `video_mask: MASK` and `audio_mask: MASK`; returns one `latent: LATENT` (`existing_video_extension.py:1163-1195`; matching cached object info VibeComfy `vibecomfy/porting/cache/object_info/ComfyUI-H3-Motion-Context-MultiRef@cpu-runtime.json:2989-3026`). At least one mask must be supplied.
- The input mask is frame-space, accepted as `[H,W]`, `[F,H,W]`, or `[F,C,H,W]`; channels in the 4-D form are averaged (`existing_video_extension.py:1105-1118`). For audio, it spatially averages each frame to `[1,1,F]`, linearly interpolates along time to the latent audio step count `T40` using `mode="linear", align_corners=False`, reshapes and broadcasts to `[1,1,2,T40]`, then clamps to `[0,1]` (`:1131-1144`). That is the **output nested noise-mask tensor** shape; the input is ordinary Comfy `MASK`. Values are noise strengths: 0 preserves encoded source audio; 1 fully denoises/regenerates it (`h3_v2v_fractional.py:443-452`). For a pure time mask, use a one-pixel-wide/high `[F,1,1]` MASK so the spatial mean is the intended value at each frame.
- Resampling is normalized linear interpolation from F frame samples to the actual VAE latent audio step count. The mask helper does not accept timestamps, sample rate or a second clock, and does not phase-align frame boundaries to 40 Hz. `FRAME_RESCALE=5/3` is the model’s video-frame to audio-latent time relationship (`comfy/ldm/minimax/model.py:30-31`); the node nevertheless resizes the supplied frame curve uniformly with align-corners false. Thus a binary edge can become fractional around the boundary. If exact tick transitions are needed, construct a 40 Hz-aligned source MASK matching the model time grid and represent it at the frame-space input resolution only if the resampler preserves the intended transitions; otherwise disclose the resampled effective curve in dry-run. The source itself provides no exact hard-boundary promise.

### Replacement versus combination and node order

- `H3V2VGranularFractionalDenoise` creates `noise_mask = NestedTensor((video_mask, audio_mask))`; its audio component is a constant full shape `(1,1,2,T40)` set from `audio_strength` (`h3_v2v_fractional.py:443-452`). Its default/current Astrid binding is `audio_strength=0` (`astrid/packs/h3_av/shared/generation.py:403-418`).
- `MiniMaxH3SetAVNoiseMask` with **only `audio_mask` supplied** reads the existing nested mask, keeps the video component unchanged, creates a new audio component from the supplied frame-space mask, and writes a new `NestedTensor`; it **replaces** the V2V audio mask, it does not multiply, max, min or add to it (`existing_video_extension.py:1204-1239`). If omitted, an existing stream is kept; if no existing mask exists, the omitted stream defaults to all-ones (fully generated). Supplying `video_mask` too also replaces video. Never pass `video_mask` for SR-5 unless intentionally replacing CC-8/V2V’s video mask.
- Ordering: target latent → `H3V2VGranularFractionalDenoise` → `MiniMaxH3SetAVNoiseMask` (audio-only) → sampler. If SetAV runs before V2V, V2V writes its constant `audio_strength` mask afterward and destroys the time-varying mask. If both are after V2V, SetAV preserves V2V’s video mask because its video input is omitted.
- To express Plan 64’s intended semantics, set V2V `audio_strength=0`, so it encodes source AV and initially preserves source audio; then set the time mask: 0 on source-kept and supplied-clip intervals, requested grant strength on generated-audio intervals, with all audio outside audio grants at 0. This lets H3 hear the source/supplied audio while generating only authorized intervals. Composition still splices fixed supplied audio exactly on its interval and selects source samples outside generated grants; the noise mask alone is not a delivery splice. A fully generated interval should be 1; fractional grant strength is supported by this node but actual quality is unverified.

### Exact SR-5 wiring replacement

> Build a frame-space temporal MASK with shape `[F,1,1]` over the full target duration. Set each frame to 0 outside generated-audio grants and on fixed supplied-audio spans; inside generated grants set the grant’s audio strength. Keep the source audio connected to `H3V2VGranularFractionalDenoise.source_audio` and bind its `audio_strength=0`. Connect its output LATENT to `MiniMaxH3SetAVNoiseMask.latent`; connect the temporal MASK to `MiniMaxH3SetAVNoiseMask.audio_mask`; leave `video_mask` disconnected. Connect SetAV’s output LATENT to the sampler’s `latent_image`. This order retains V2V’s video mask and replaces its constant audio mask with the time-varying one. The node averages mask pixels, linearly resamples F frame values to the target latent’s T40 audio steps with align-corners false, and broadcasts to `[1,1,2,T40]`; record that effective resampled mask separately from requested grants and delivered PCM spans. Composition retains source PCM outside grants and splices supplied clips on their own intervals. Never put SetAV before V2V, and never supply its `video_mask` in this task.

## Genuine owner decisions

**Audio boundary precision:** the motion node linearly resamples a frame-space curve onto 40 Hz audio latents, so a requested binary edge may become fractional around the boundary. Code establishes the mechanics but cannot decide whether SR-5’s product promise requires a hard tick boundary in the latent mask.

- Option A: accept linear interpolation at the model-conditioning boundary; retain exact delivered PCM outside grants through composition; show the effective resampled mask in dry-run. Recommendation: A, since the task’s exactness requirement is delivery ownership and this is the pinned node’s existing input contract.
- Option B: require exact 40 Hz latent transitions; add a tick-addressed mask contract/lowering or change the node input to carry audio-clock timing, and refuse the current frame-space approximation until that exists. This is more exact but expands scope beyond SR-5 and needs a node/API design.

The other choices—soft guide versus exact ownership, default `hold: chunk` plus optional `hold: frame`, keeping the #863 workaround, no shot writeback, and using an audio-only override after V2V—are already stated or decided in the plans. The report proposes JSON hole markers and collision errors as implementation-contract clarifications, not product behavior requiring owner judgment.
