# Tasks — A living planet on the vision page

[Plan](plan.md) · [Inputs](inputs.md)

**Task ids:** `PL-1` to `PL-10`, `R`. **Critical path:** PL-1, PL-2, PL-3, PL-8, PL-9, R. **Test inventory:** in astrid-app, the `vision-content` job in `.github/workflows/public-vision.yml`, `tests/e2e/public-astrid-vision-captures.spec.ts` (pictures of the hero, Pillars and Principles), `tests/e2e/public-astrid-vision-entry.spec.ts` (it reads the sky canvas's pixels, near 418) and the page's mobile, layout and polish tests. Paths and line numbers were read on astrid-app `release/15/main` on 22 November 2026 and are re-checked at dispatch.

**How this plan runs.** The manager judges by eye. Every task that changes what a visitor sees returns pictures at the five sizes in [plan.md](plan.md#completion), before and after, taken with the planet's time frozen so two pictures can be compared, plus a short recording where motion is the point. The manager accepts, or sends it back with what is wrong, and writes its judgment beside the pictures. A worker does not decide that something looks good enough.

Every card: the words of the vision, pillars and principles are not changed; no other page changes; never edit, skip or weaken an existing test to pass. The page's screenshot baselines are existing assertions: they change once, in PL-8, on the owner's approval of the new look, and nowhere else.
