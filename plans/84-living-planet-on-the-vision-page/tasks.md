## Human feedback and genuine creative decision gates

**Use the [Plan 84 ChatGPT orchestration guide](chatgpt-orchestration.md) as the sole procedure.** Every important visual feedback request must include an **actual relevant reference image next to the current result** (and the real website-size view), not a reference filename and a generic question; see [reference-versus-result bundles](chatgpt-orchestration.md#make-every-feedback-request-reviewable-in-one-glance). Share verified proof and a real feedback destination at meaningful stages; do **not** repeatedly ask the owner to sign off on routine code or test changes. Each internally accepted SC/FL/CAM creative unit gets an owner-visible screenshot/clip, 2–3 specific optional questions and the next task. Technical milestones send a concise evidence digest; avoid noisy prompts unless a genuine tradeoff is visible.

**Four human decisions are deliberately blocking for their dependent art only:** `VG-STYLE` (planet and mink visual identity), `VG-STORY` (first collaborative motion/personality), `VG-WORLD` (whole 360° composition), and `VG-MOTION` (cinematic entrance language). Share bilingual English/Turkish questions and explicit **Approve / Request changes / Alternative** options at those gates. The owner can interrupt or redirect anywhere. Record exactly which evidence/commit the decision covers. If the owner has not replied, the gate remains pending; **continue all unrelated ready work**, never silently approve.

At ordinary unit completion, the manager/reviewer accepts the result internally and moves forward without waiting; any later human feedback becomes focused rework. The separate initial run authority and final release/publication approval are never bypassed by this feedback policy.

## Creative direction and acceptance

Every meaningful creative object has its **own** task and verdict; technical helpers can be subdivided or grouped freely. Read [creative-units.md](creative-units.md) for the art brief, graybox, on-page still, short clip, signature activity and no-clutter rules. One approved scene is not proof of eight; and eight separate successful scenes are not proof of a coherent globe. The creative manager and independent critic perform their internal reviews. Share verified creative results and optional questions with the owner after each SC/FL/CAM unit; for the four load-bearing VG decisions above, dependent art waits for explicit owner direction while independent work continues. Show useful batches and full-globe compositions without making each individual scene another human approval gate. Never force all eight scenes into the visible hemisphere or all four flying objects into the sky simultaneously.

## Exact time and interaction witnesses

**Math:** use a pure mapping between an **unwrapped yaw phase** and 24-hour effective time. A 2π horizontal turn equals 24 hours; wrap hours to [0,24) for display/session. Fix an art-directed yaw reference at a named reference hour. Rightward screen movement increases hours, left decreases. Keep pitch independent: no inference of time from the final quaternion where mixed pitch could affect yaw. Set one selected hour from the shared sky session at initial mount.

**Input:** start gesture only after an intentional horizontal threshold; vertical-only pointer/touch movement may adjust bounded pitch only where it doesn't steal scroll. Diagonal motion changes time from horizontal displacement alone. Keyboard alternatives for earlier/later and reset-to-live; no time changes from merely hovering, camera animations, planet idle or flying objects.

**Authority and echo:** all external `getSnapshot().hours`/preview/replay updates set planet yaw and lighting without calling `setManualHours`. Only an explicit horizontal user turn writes to the shared sky session, coalesced to at most one update per rendered change. `setNow()` resets manual pin; restore correct local hour and yaw. Verify real-time mode resumes clock updates and both Home and Vision show identical page state.

**Regression examples:** at 06:00, 12:00, 18:00 and 00:00 facing changes reproducibly; from 23:30, a 2-hour right turn reaches 01:30, the same left turn from 01:30 goes to 23:30. Drag up/down 200px with dx=0 → **zero changes** to time/palette; dragging right changes both planet and page. External manual hour update/replay → one planet update, **zero echo callbacks**. Entry, exit and flying object motion → **zero time writes**. Test with fixed timezone/date and around sunrise and sunset.

## Visual and polish approval

**Art gate:** a plain-material globe looks rounded and inhabited beyond the visible hemisphere. The icon-like 3D mink matches the Pixiemink silhouette and cream/dark-tip markings at reading size. One convincing workshop scene receives an internal creative acceptance verdict before the seven other individually reviewed scenes, and its owner-facing images and questions are sent without waiting for a response.

**Cinematic entry:** the heading/CTA are immediately usable; the poster holds until the canvas is ready; camera follows a short controlled approach and arc into the **time-selected** end view. The sphere does not sweep its longitude or advance the clock to fake a camera move. Coordinate with existing Home/Vision transitions and the 1.8-second sky reveal, but do not require a fixed duration until video review.

**Exit:** leave Vision with an appropriate reverse camera travel or measured dissolve, with no navigation delay, stuck canvas, time reset or forced scroll. Back/Forward, interruptions and repeated navigation are tested; reduced-motion users skip spatial fly-through.

**Flying details:** four separately art-directed aerial candidates have authored physical motion around/above the globe, correct occlusion and day/night lighting, independent of clock time. No dramatic high-speed crossing in front of the headline. Limit density; pause offscreen/reduced motion and include the fallback still.

## Delivery rules

One accountable project owner; no second queue of tasks. Record stage evidence and approved art images/recordings before moving forward. Tests and proof run against pinned source, seed, viewport, time and device profile. The owner may redirect or pause at any stage, but silence never counts as art approval or a blocker. Creative reviewers and targeted checks still maintain quality; extra polish does not replace failed core geometry, time math or character identity.

Do not edit, skip or weaken existing website tests. Maintain known rights and editable source for 3D models, textures and animations. Integration/publication still require current release authority and source SHAs.
