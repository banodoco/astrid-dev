# Tasks — A living planet on the vision page

[Plan](plan.md) · [Inputs](inputs.md)

**Task ids:** `PL-0` to `PL-9`, `R`. **Critical path:** PL-0, PL-1, PL-2, PL-6, PL-7, PL-8, R. **Test inventory:** in astrid-app, the `vision-content` job in `.github/workflows/public-vision.yml` (near 64–109; its path filter, near 4–58, does not yet include the planet's files), `tests/e2e/public-astrid-vision-captures.spec.ts` (near 25–53; it writes pictures at two sizes and compares them with nothing), `tests/e2e/public-astrid-vision-entry.spec.ts` (near 97–99 it pins the headline's geometry, near 418 it reads the sky canvas's pixels), the phone layout and dialog test (`mobile-catalogs`, near 51–74), the test that pins both section links (`sigils-nav`, near 294–300) and `polish.spec.ts` (near 233–258, contrast over plain paper). Paths and line numbers were read on astrid-app `release/15/main` on 22 November 2026 and are re-checked at dispatch.

**How this plan runs.** The manager judges by eye. Every task that changes what a visitor sees returns pictures at the five sizes in [plan.md](plan.md#completion), before and after, taken with the planet in a fixed pose and with the viewport, pixel density, content feed, sky hour and drawing backend pinned, so two pictures can be compared, plus a short recording where motion is the point. A browser for this is available in the cloud session. The manager accepts, or sends it back with what is wrong, and writes its judgment beside the pictures. A worker does not decide that something looks good enough.

Every card: the words of the vision, pillars and principles are not changed; choosing a pillar card still opens its goals, gaps and projects; both section headings and both section links stay; no other page changes. Never edit, skip or weaken an existing test to pass: if the new layout cannot satisfy an existing assertion, that is brought to the owner, not changed.
