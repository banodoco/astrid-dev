# Decisions ledger: <run or contributor>, <date>

Use when product work proceeds while the owner cannot be asked (for example, a contributor helping while the owner is offline). Keep one ledger per run, next to the run's evidence. The owner reconciles it later, row by row: accept, change or revert.

Types:
- **override**: deviated from the written plan.
- **guess**: the plan was silent or ambiguous, and we picked.
- **STOP-proposal**: the plan says stop and ask; we recorded both sides, chose a resolution and kept going.
- **improvement**: a suggested change to a plan or process; not built unless approved.
