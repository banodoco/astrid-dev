After a day of dispatch, about a dozen recurring Lead rulings lived only in mailbox comments, so a fresh Lead, Orchestrator or packet session could not find them. Each ruling now lives where its reader looks.

**In skills, for every release:**
- **Green 3A:** a packet is judged on its own change, and a flip is told by three isolated runs on each side.
- **Lead workstream:** a gate packet's brief states its threat model; check for pin changes before merging and re-point pins after a squash; brief reviewers to read hosted runs; read every Review the Orchestrator reports; schedule check-ins outside the machine.
- **Orchestrator workstream:** a relay counts only when confirmed; replacements carry the open decisions; check the account's session limit; it may dispatch a workflow a session cannot.
- **Packet workstream:** read the mailbox first; merge the destination head before final proof; one hosted run at a time per branch; pin changes in their own commit.

**On the board:** the Release 14 rulings are in a Standing rulings section, grouped by reader, and the run template gains that section.

A fresh-context review checked the edits for priority and level. It moved the general rulings into their skills and led to two more changes: packet sessions are now told the rulings bind them, and the Orchestrator's workflow permission is stated in Authority.
