Plans can now tag a task with a fourth route, `T` for taste: small, editorial refinement that needs a high level of polish (user-facing copy, refusal and error messages, names, a screen's layout, a skill's voice). A `T` task refines something that exists against a stated intent, adds no scope, and proves itself with the exact before and after against the taste rules. It runs on the high-judgment class. The run contract owns the definition; the plan and manage skills, the run template, route recording and the completion check accept it.
