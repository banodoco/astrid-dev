Every packet review had gone to the most capable model. The Lead workstream now matches the reviewer to the risk:
- **Fast model:** mechanical checks.
- **Mid model:** bounded fixes with a clear oracle, run on both base and head.
- **High-judgment model:** gates, designs, new subsystems, concurrency, custody, security, and the first packet of a kind. A large or contract-touching mid-model "merge" goes up to it.

The injected-failure rule for gates stays. The skill gains about 90 words.
