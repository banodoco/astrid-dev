How: `validate/check.sh <file> <name>` measures each output: duration, codec, loudness, silence gaps, frame count and frame-to-frame change. It also writes a picture: a spectrogram for audio, a 4×2 contact sheet for video, the image itself for stills. I look at the picture. ## Verdict classes (Hannah, 04:20: "some issues with outputs may be raw model limitations too")
Every output that doesn't do what was asked gets one of three classes before anyone fixes anything:
1. **Pipeline fault:** our product broke the request: wrong inputs bound, a reference not passed, wrong settings or resolution, truncated audio, a failed compose. This is a release issue, routed to its owner under "root or logged".
2. **Model limitation:** the pipeline delivered the request faithfully and the model itself falls short: weak prompt adherence, artifacts, identity drift. Evidence: the inputs reached the graph as declared (logs and the compiled plan); hivemind or community reports of the same behaviour; or a different seed or settings showing the same thing. This is not a release blocker. It's recorded as a known limitation for the user-facing notes, plus a better default or guidance if there's a cheap one.
3. **Unclear:** not enough context to tell. This is exactly the context-capture gap Hannah asked about, so the Fable diagnosis lead proposes what the product should record (declared-logs pattern) so the next one is clear.
