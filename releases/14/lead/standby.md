# Release 14 standby Lead

The owner keeps a standby Lead in a Codex session (Astra), so that Release 14 keeps moving if the Claude Lead session stops. The standby waits, and takes over only after 3 hours without a Lead heartbeat. The general rule is in the [Lead workstream](../../../skills/roles/astrid-dev-lead/workstreams/multi-manager-release.md#standby-lead).

## Heartbeat

The Claude Lead posts on [mailbox #796](https://github.com/banodoco/astrid-dev/issues/796) at least every 90 minutes, a `STATUS` when nothing else is due. A **Claude Lead message** is a comment whose `text` block says `from_session: r14-lead` and whose body ends with the footer `_Generated by [Claude Code](https://claude.ai/code)_`.

## Wait mode (every hour)

**Is a takeover already in force?** Check this first. A takeover is a mailbox comment that begins its body with `Lead continuity: the standby Lead session is now r14-lead` and has no Claude footer. A handback is a later comment beginning `Lead continuity: handback`.
- If the newest of the two is your own takeover, you are the Lead: run check-ins, not wait mode.
- If the newest is a takeover by another session, stop. Only one standby exists at a time, and the owner retires the old one when she starts a new one.

**Find the newest Claude Lead message.**
- It may be of any type: STATUS, DECISION, DONE or REQUEST.
- Its fenced `text` header contains `run: r14-dispatch`, `from: lead` and `from_session: r14-lead`.
- Its body ends, after trailing whitespace, with `_Generated by [Claude Code](https://claude.ai/code)_`.
- It is posted by the Lead's GitHub account, `AstridPseudon`.

Start at the last page and page backwards until you find one. The last page is ⌈comments ÷ 100⌉ from `gh api repos/banodoco/astrid-dev/issues/796 --jq .comments`. Read pages with `gh api "repos/banodoco/astrid-dev/issues/796/comments?per_page=100&page=<p>"`, or the same through your GitHub connection. Use UTC `created_at`.

- **Under 3 hours old:** do nothing and post nothing.
- **3 hours or more:** rule out a quiet Lead. Look for activity by `AstridPseudon` in the same 3 hours in banodoco/astrid-dev, astrid-agent, astrid-runtime, astrid-app or VibeComfy:
  - a pull request opened or merged;
  - a PR or issue comment;
  - a push to a `claude/*` branch;
  - a commit to `releases/14/run.md` on main.

  If there is any, wait another hour. If there is none, re-read the mailbox tail once more, then take over.
- **You cannot tell** (pages unreadable, rate-limited, no Lead message found at all): do not take over. Tell the owner in your chat, and retry next hour.

## If the Claude Lead comes back

After a takeover, the Claude Lead stands down. It posts nothing and acts on nothing until the owner hands the role back. The handback is a mailbox comment that begins `Lead continuity: handback to <session>`, posted by the active Lead when the owner asks it to. The standby stays Lead until then.
