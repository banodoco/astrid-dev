# Release 12 — Reliable updates and one generation path

**Release:** 12
**State:** Draft

## Highlight

Updating Astrid works reliably on every platform, and creators generate media through one path, locally or on a rented GPU they control.

## Intent

Release 12 began as the release that makes updating dependable: the fixes from Release 11's field failure, the review-round repairs, and every product regression found against Release 10. On 10 Nov 2026 Hannah folded the generation work into the same release, rather than holding it for a Release 13. That work is:
- one generation path ([Plan 32](../plans/32-generation-placement-targets/plan.md));
- rented-machine lifetime and cost controls ([Plan 40](../plans/40-machine-lifetime-controls/plan.md));
- the first-image onboarding pieces that depend on it ([Plan 20](../plans/20-zero-to-first-image/plan.md), phase 1.5 and staged work);
- their live GPU proof ([Plan 34](../plans/34-real-gpu-proof/plan.md)).

Both halves are close to done, and shipping them together means one qualification, one candidate and one publish instead of two.

**A good outcome:**
- one published tuple (Agent, App, Runtime, plus the VibeComfy and runpod-lifecycle pins) that passes the Release 12 gate;
- every default generation slot is proven on a real GPU through the product;
- Hannah confirms the update works on her Mac through the normal App path.

## Approach

The release runs as **two tracks that merge once**:

| Track | What it finishes | Document |
|---|---|---|
| **A — Fixes** | The Release 12 candidate `cand/29-r12`: Agent fixes, App Direct Journey, harness, matrix | [releases/12/fixes.md](12/fixes.md) |
| **B — Generation** | Plans 32, 40 and 20 integrated into one generation candidate, proven on CPU and on GPU (Plan 34) | [releases/12/generation.md](12/generation.md) |

Track A keeps going exactly as it was. Track B finishes its plans on its own integration branch. When both reach their checkpoint, **Track B merges into Track A's candidate**. The merged candidate is qualified once, as one tuple, and published.

**Keep the merge cheap.** Track B rehearses merging Track A's latest Agent candidate every time it moves, on a separate rehearsal branch, as Plan 32 already does with `plan32/r12-reconcile`. The final merge is then a known quantity, and conflicts surface while both tracks still have time.

### Gate (both tracks, merged tuple)

1. The full `journey.yml` matrix, row=direct, all 11 rows green.
2. No regression against R10 `72462b04`, served R11 `a105de3a` / `4f5477cb` or `main`. D8 applies: product regressions against R10 are fixed here; stale or test-infra failures go to Plan 39.
3. App Tests and Direct Journey with 0 failures on the pinned App head.
4. Release review rounds done with no open blocker, including one round on the generation merge delta.
5. **Generation proof:** each default generation slot passes on a real GPU through the product, on the final tuple or on a generation candidate with no generation-affecting change since. The pod's VibeComfy HEAD is verified to match the pin.

**The bar** (Hannah): fix and question every failure; never weaken a check. "Test drift" is proven from the trace or it isn't drift.

### CI and runners

The Release 12 runner freeze existed so that other work wouldn't starve Release 12. Generation is now Release 12, so the freeze is replaced by **scheduling**:
- Track A's runs have priority.
- Track B runs **one** Agent CI comparison on its integrated generation candidate, instead of separate Plan 32 and Plan 40 comparisons. That run satisfies their CI gates if it contains their heads.
- Nothing else pushes to `int/**` or dispatches CI until publish.
- Runtime and VibeComfy run CI on every push, so push to them only when the release needs it.

The reviewed workflow prep that drops `int/**` push triggers (`plan32/release*-ci-prep`) may land in the candidate if the orchestrator chooses. Otherwise it follows after publish.

### Who decides what

- **The orchestrator decides:** ordering, routing, merge timing, the CI schedule, budget splits within caps, and which track owns a fix.
- **Hannah decides:**
  - **If one track is ready long before the other:** wait, or ship Track A alone as Release 12 and the generation work next. *Recommendation:* decide at checkpoint A with an honest estimate for B; don't decide early.
  - **GPU spend** above the current $20 stop for the final-tuple GPU proof.
  - Publication, under her standing go once the gate holds.
