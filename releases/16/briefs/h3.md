## Outcome

- **For creators and agents:** one request describes a shot (what to keep exactly, what to generate or edit, how to steer), at whole-shot and frame level.
- **Placements, not modes:** continue, bridge and insert are placements of clips or frames on the timeline.
- **Exact means exact:** kept pixels and samples are verified in the final file.
- **The dry run tells the truth first:** before any GPU spend it states what is exact, soft, verified or untested, the cost, and the nearest option to any refusal.
- **For developers:** a new capability is one ordinary pack, a pinned node repository plus one YAML file per contribution and a hand-written skill section. It stacks with everything else through typed sockets, footprints and a test kit, with no Astrid engineering.
- **In Release 17,** one request can run several passes (de-rope, edit-then-extend, refine, repair).

## How it runs

**Option:** packets

- **Start.** Open the lane board `board: lane h3` first, then post one STATUS. The startup checks, each posted as one STATUS line, passed or failed: the brief is on `main` and its `**Go:**` is not `Pending` with State `running`; the plan branches in the Plans and order table exist at their SHAs; `python3 scripts/astrid_dev.py release status 16` and `release status 17` read cleanly.
- **Dispatch.** The orchestrator writes each unit's packet at dispatch from the unit's cards: outcome, May touch (the card's Scope), focused proof, base SHA and the target `release/<N>/plan-<PP>`. No packet files are prepared ahead. The older `releases/16/packets/R16-P66-*`, `-P67-*`, `-P68-*` and `-P70-*` files are history only. No in-flight cap or first-unit-alone rule is set in this brief; a unit is dispatched once its Depends are merged. Route and mode per unit: one fresh normal ChatGPT session per unit, in N High, X Extra High or S Pro as the unit's card names. T editorial work uses the run's taste binding. Replacement: findings go back to the same author once, then to a fresh session; a unit parked after one replacement is [Owner only](#owner-only). The check-in cadence is in Check-ins.
- **Proof.** The author runs the focused proof, pushes `p<PP>/<unit>`, and opens a PR into the plan branch; green CI plus the shown proof is the evidence. Once Plan 66 CC-14 lands, its `engine_deps` lane is the one proof workflow for every unit and pack. A unit may land as several PRs. There is no separate proof custodian.
- **Reviews.**
  - The reviewer's ACCEPT is required at Plan 66 RS1 (job-record half, then contract half), on every S-route unit, at Plan 67 R (the programme's integrated review) and at Plan 107 R. Other units merge on green CI and shown proof.
  - Findings become rows `R<n>.<m>` under the reviewed card. They go back to the same author once, then to a fresh session.
- **Merges.**
  - The orchestrator merges `release/<N>/main` forward into each plan branch as it moves.
  - When a plan's units are merged, and its review stop accepted where it has one, it merges the plan branch into `release/<N>/main`. It does this after any overlapping merge from other lanes. This is the release gate: `orchestrator, after any overlapping merge`, in force from the owner's go on this brief.
  - It cuts later branches itself with `git push` from the base named in the release brief.
- **Check-ins.** One STATUS on the board per check-in: units merged, units running, blocked items with their cause, and the next units.

## Roles

- **Orchestrator:** answers to the owner for this lane, not to the Release 16 Lead. Signs its comments `h3-orchestrator`. It may:
  - cut and merge plan branches;
  - dispatch, resume and replace author and reviewer sessions;
  - post on boards.
  - One orchestrator runs the whole H3 programme from this brief. There are no per-plan managers; the per-plan manager briefs (`manager-P66`, `manager-P67`, `manager-P68`, `manager-P70`) were superseded on 28 November 2026.
- **Manager or packet sessions:** one fresh normal ChatGPT session per unit (the packet authors in How it runs), bounded to that unit's card. They may write and prove their unit and open a PR into its plan branch; they never merge into a plan branch or `release/<N>/main`, and they never write another unit's card.
- **Reviewer:** [reviewer-h3.md](reviewer-h3.md); one session per review stop or S-route unit, started by the orchestrator.
- **Advisor:** a delegate asks the orchestrator. The orchestrator asks the Release 16 Lead for release-level questions, and the reviewer for technical judgment beyond its own route. The orchestrator is never anyone's advisor except its own delegates'.
- **Owner:** see [Owner only](#owner-only).
