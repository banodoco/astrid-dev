## What “dependable” means

A person and their agent can install, connect, discover or prepare generation, generate, import real footage, edit and save safely, render the correct work, recover from ordinary failures, and update or roll back safely without improvising system administration. Critical blockers include data loss/corruption, wrong output, failure of a promised supported path, unconsented actions, uncontrolled expenditure, stuck unrecoverable states, and unusably slow operations that prevent the journey. General speed, pleasant collaboration UX, extensibility, structural cleanup and optional new features belong later unless required for this promise. Preserve existing safe behavior and do not make dependability synonymous with adding many features.
