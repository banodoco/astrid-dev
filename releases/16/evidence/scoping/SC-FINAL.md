A short credible execution sequence is:

1. Once scope/dispatch authority exists, refresh the predecessor and owner choices; start inventories, fixtures, current-source donor analysis, native/managed feasibility, control preparation and save measurements together.
2. Settle and review only the consumed provider/closure, target/plan/grant, outcome/custody and update/publication interfaces. Producer and consumer preparation can run concurrently; affected consumer landing waits for its actual interface.
3. Exercise the two independent internal vertical witnesses as soon as their narrow seams are usable, alongside native/managed/old-origin work. Neither witness waits for all of P35/P38 or for the other witness.
4. Converge retained managed/native, state/migration, substantial/history-bearing, render and bounded adjunct work. Finish required code/interface reviews and rehearse the freeze before scarce final proof.
5. One integrator freezes the consumed tuple in producer order. Qualify accepted support and negative rows, heavy and timing-sensitive journeys, effective checks and the complete comparator on that exact tuple. Complete RL-R/final qualification reviews, then obtain the exact owner go and perform the one protected publication/readback.
