# Release 13 — Control over rented GPU time

**Release:** 13
**State:** Draft

## Highlight

Creators can see and control when their rented GPU machine stops and how much it can cost.

## Intent

Make paid, remotely rented generation less surprising. When someone launches a RunPod machine, they should understand its lifetime, accrued cost and rate, and be able to extend it without losing control over their spending. This is an advance release brief: its scope can change before final qualification.

## Approach

Start with [Plan 40](../plans/40-machine-lifetime-controls/plan.md)'s lifetime and spend-cap controls. Make remaining time, cost to date and hourly price visible, and require fresh consent within a user-set cap before extension. Confirm the behavior on the actual integrated release candidate and keep other plans out until they are deliberately added. This file does **not** mean Release 13 is approved, pinned, or published.

## Included plans

- [Plan 40](../plans/40-machine-lifetime-controls/plan.md) — Make rented machine lifetime, remaining time, cost and explicit extension controls understandable and enforceable.
