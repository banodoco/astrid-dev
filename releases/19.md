# Release 19 — Version 0.1, the stable release

**Release:** 19
**State:** Draft

## Highlight

Version 0.1: on Windows, macOS and Linux a creator installs, generates image, video and audio quickly on their own ComfyUI, edits with an agent, exports a video and updates without losing work.

## Intent

The first version to call stable. A good outcome: testers run the whole path unaided on the three systems, every test is green, and the documentation takes a new person from purpose to finished work.

Proposed by the advisor and agreed by Hannah on 15 November 2026 ("those releases make sense"), with fixes and plans added on the fly from actual usage and user reports. See [Strategy](../strategy.md#decisions).

## Approach

Hardening only: no new features. Fix what the tester runs find, finish the documentation, and declare honestly what is not supported. Open owner decision: whether rented machines are fully supported in 0.1 or labelled experimental with their money-safety fixes done.

Each release has one theme, plus a standing lane for fixes and small plans that come from actual usage and user reports.
