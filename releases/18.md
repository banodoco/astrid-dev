# Release 18 — Finish a video

**Release:** 18
**State:** Draft

## Highlight

A creator takes a video from first generation to export, with an agent editing alongside them without ever overwriting their work.

## Intent

Generation alone is not a creative tool. A good outcome: generate straight onto the timeline, agent edits that respect the creator's edits and show conflicts, and export with sensible presets.

Proposed by the advisor and agreed by Hannah on 15 November 2026 ("those releases make sense"), with fixes and plans added on the fly from actual usage and user reports. See [Strategy](../strategy.md#decisions).

## Approach

Three of the four plans are goals without tasks. Before this release, during Release 15 or 16, one real video is taken from first generation to export and everything that breaks is written down; that list is this release's scope.

Each release has one theme, plus a standing lane for fixes and small plans that come from actual usage and user reports.

## Included plans

- [Plan 37](../plans/37-generate-time-destination/plan.md) — a generation placed on the timeline in the same request.
- [Plan 04](../plans/04-concurrent-edits/plan.md) — agent edits that never silently overwrite the creator's.
- [Plan 18](../plans/18-timeline-editing/plan.md) — reliable timeline editing.
- [Plan 27](../plans/27-video-render-options/plan.md) — export with presets and control.
