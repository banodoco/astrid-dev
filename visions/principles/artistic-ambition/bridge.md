# Bridge — Built for Artistic Ambition

**Purpose:** Translate [the principle](principle.md) into product judgment and a possible proof, without creating a second implementation plan.

## Existing foundations

Astrid already combines a video editor, agent-driven timeline work, generation/processing packs and editable creative artifacts. Existing plans strengthen [timeline editing](../../../plans/18-timeline-editing/plan.md), [generation](../../../plans/02-consistent-generation/plan.md) and [creative-workflow extensibility](../../../plans/17-vibecomfy-extensibility/plan.md). These are inputs, **not evidence that an ambitious end-to-end artistic workflow is already solved**.

## What must be proven

- **End-to-end depth:** A person can pursue a substantial project with shot/scene continuity, control and meaningful revision, rather than stitching together one-off demonstrations.
- **Artistic control:** Outputs are inspectable and editable, and agents preserve explicit artistic decisions across iterations.
- **Rising capability:** A novice or experienced artist can take on a self-chosen challenge that would previously have been out of reach.
- **Artistic quality:** Evaluate resulting work with creator-defined goals, serious human critique and real examples—not throughput metrics alone.

## First proof

Support one ambitious artist-led project from concept through repeated creation, editing, review and final presentation. Preserve changes, allow fine-grained revision and record where the artist has to fight the software. Judge the result against the artist's intention and an independent creative review.

This bridge names a product-level acceptance question. Scope, owner, deliverables, evidence and execution remain with [Astrid Dev plans](../../../plans/) and the relevant product repositories.
