# Bridge — Collective Ambition

**Purpose:** Connect [the principle](principle.md) to concrete cultural and ownership mechanisms. This is not a community program, legal instrument, equity offer or approved project plan.

## What to build on

The Banodoco/Astrid creative community and its creative competitions, as described by the founder, are existing starting points. [Astrid Dev](../../../README.md) already organizes contributions to product development; [Liquid Software](../liquid-software/principle.md) and [Collective Intelligence](../collective-intelligence/principle.md) make creative/tool and knowledge contributions more reusable.

These foundations do not themselves establish a contributor-ownership company, entitlement to equity or an effective incentive program.

## Three practical workstreams

| Goal | Concrete mechanism to explore | First evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **An excuse to attempt more** | Art competitions, demanding briefs, shared creative challenges, showcases and collaborations | Creators attempt and finish work they would not otherwise have started |
| **A culture that raises the bar** | Peer critique, high-quality examples, mentorship, recognition for originality and difficult work | Creators' ambitions and artistic outcomes improve across successive projects |
| **Ownership tied to contribution** | A publicly understandable framework for giving **100% of company ownership** to people who advance the collective | A reviewed, legally workable ownership/governance proposal with contributor categories, eligibility, allocation, vesting, rights and the treatment of funding |

## Decisions not to invent in a vision document

Who qualifies as advancing the collective, what counts as meaningful contribution, how equity is allocated over time, whether rights differ by contributor type, and how prior or future financing fits the 100% goal require explicit owner decisions and professional legal/tax review.

The underlying design requirement is simple: **the people responsible for pushing this mission forward should own the company behind it**. Do not reduce this to competition prizes or generic engagement points, and do not describe unspecified legal structures as established facts.

## Boundary to plans

Artistic programming and contributor governance are distinct deliverables requiring separate ownership and proof. They should connect back to this principle rather than become additional top-level vision pillars. Neither is currently authorized for execution by this document.
