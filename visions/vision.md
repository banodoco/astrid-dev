# Astrid — A tool to unlock the collective artistic ambition of the open-source community

Astrid aims to help people pursue work beyond what they thought possible.

## Four pillars

1. **[A Tool for Artistic Ambition](pillars/artistic-ambition.md)** — We want to make it easier for artists to push their work—and themselves—toward ever more ambitious creative possibilities.
2. **[Agent-Powered Liquid Software](pillars/liquid-software.md)** — In the age of agents, we believe every layer of software should adapt to the needs of the artist and the work in front of them.
3. **[Intelligence That Compounds](pillars/compounding-intelligence.md)** — We want everything artists and their agents learn to help others push their work further.
4. **[Shared Creative Endeavour](pillars/shared-endeavour.md)** — We want to build a culture of people pushing AI—and themselves—beyond their creative limits.

The collective inspires ambitious work; adaptable tools and shared knowledge help realize it; results raise the bar and improve the foundation for everyone. The stated goal is **100% ownership of the company behind the collective by those advancing its mission**. The actual allocation and legal structure remain to be designed.

## How we build

Three [cross-cutting principles](principles.md) apply across the pillars: **local-first, cloud-capable; open by design; creator-controlled**. They describe constraints on our approach, not extra creative pillars.

**[Gaps](README.md)** record what the present system does not yet demonstrate relative to *either* the pillars or these principles. **[Plans](../plans/)** target those gaps; evidence updates our understanding. A completed plan is not an automatically closed gap.

## Build Astrid with us

You and your agents can contribute to Astrid's tools, knowledge and creative community. We want people who meaningfully advance this mission to share in ownership of the company behind it. The ownership structure is still being worked out.
