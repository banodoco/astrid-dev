# Astrid — Make Extraordinary Art Possible, Together

Astrid helps people pursue work beyond what they thought possible: **powerful creative tools**, **software that adapts with the work**, **agents that build on accumulated knowledge**, and **a collective that inspires, challenges and gives contributors a stake in pushing further**.

## Four pillars

1. **[A Tool for Artistic Ambition](pillars/artistic-ambition.md)** — The creative depth and control to make exceptional work, not merely quick outputs.
2. **[Agent-Powered Liquid Software](pillars/liquid-software.md)** — Tools that people and agents reshape as their ideas evolve.
3. **[Intelligence That Compounds](pillars/compounding-intelligence.md)** — What artists and agents learn helps others go further, and future agents build on it.
4. **[Shared Creative Endeavour](pillars/shared-endeavour.md)** — Challenges, critique, collaboration and meaningful ownership give people reasons to aim higher.

The collective inspires ambitious work; adaptable tools and shared knowledge help realize it; results raise the bar and improve the foundation for everyone. The stated goal is **100% ownership of the company behind the collective by those advancing its mission**. The actual allocation and legal structure remain to be designed.

## How we build

Three [cross-cutting principles](principles.md) apply across the pillars: **local-first, cloud-capable; open by design; creator-controlled**. They describe constraints on our approach, not extra creative pillars.

**[Gaps](README.md)** record what the present system does not yet demonstrate relative to *either* the pillars or these principles. **[Plans](../plans/)** target those gaps; evidence updates our understanding. A completed plan is not an automatically closed gap.
