# Astrid — Make Extraordinary Art Possible, Together

Astrid brings together **the creative power to pursue ambitious ideas**, **software that adapts as you work**, **agents that learn from collective experience**, and **a community that gives people the inspiration, challenges and shared ownership to push further**.

The aim is not simply to make creative work faster. It is to raise what people dare to attempt, what they can accomplish and what they inspire others to make.

## Four reinforcing principles

1. **[Built for Artistic Ambition](principles/artistic-ambition/principle.md)** — Tools with the depth, control and quality to pursue exceptional creative work, not just quick results.
2. **[Agent-Powered Liquid Software](principles/liquid-software/principle.md)** — People and agents continually adapt, assemble and extend the tools around the evolving work.
3. **[Collective Intelligence That Compounds](principles/collective-intelligence/principle.md)** — Agents learn from their own experience and shared community knowledge, applying what works and contributing new understanding.
4. **[Collective Ambition, Shared Ownership](principles/collective-ambition/principle.md)** — Artistic challenges, collaboration, critique and recognition give people a reason to aim higher. The stated goal is that **100% of the company behind the collective belongs to the people advancing its mission**.

## One reinforcing cycle

The collective inspires a more ambitious goal. Powerful, adaptable tools and shared knowledge help make it real. The resulting work raises expectations; lessons improve the tools and knowledge available to others; contributors share in the collective they advance.

## From vision to practice

Each principle has a short statement, a **[bridge](README.md)** from present reality to concrete proofs and relevant plans, and optional references for deeper design and source evidence. **[Implementation plans](../plans/)** own work, priority, authorization and acceptance. Plans link back to the principles they advance; these links express purpose, not permission to start work.

The ownership statement is a **goal, not a description of an already implemented legal allocation**. Eligibility, governance, financing and legal mechanics require separate decisions.

**Our measure is the extraordinary work people can actually create together.**
