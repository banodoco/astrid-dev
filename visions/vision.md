# Astrid — A tool to unlock the collective artistic ambition of the open-source community

Astrid aims to help individuals and the community as a whole pursue artistic work beyond what they thought possible.

The information on this page is pulled from the Astrid Dev repository. If you'd like to contribute, visit [the contribution guide](https://github.com/banodoco/astrid-dev#want-to-contribute-give-this-to-your-agent).

## Four pillars

1. **[A Tool for Artistic Ambition](pillars/artistic-ambition.md)** — We want to make it easier for artists to push their work—and themselves—toward more ambitious projects.
2. **[Agent-Powered Liquid Software](pillars/liquid-software.md)** — In the age of agents, we believe every layer of software should adapt to the needs of the artist and the work.
3. **[Intelligence That Compounds](pillars/compounding-intelligence.md)** — We want everything artists and their agents learn to help others push their work further.
4. **[A Shared Creative Endeavor](pillars/shared-endeavour.md)** — We want to build a culture of people pushing AI—and themselves—beyond their creative limits.

The collective inspires ambitious work; adaptable tools and shared knowledge help realize it; results raise the bar and improve the foundation for everyone. The stated goal is **100% ownership of the company behind the collective by those advancing its mission**. The actual allocation and legal structure remain to be designed.

## The principles that drive us

These four [guiding principles](principles.md) describe what we're building toward across every pillar: **local-first, cloud-capable; open by design; in the creator's control; eliminate needless technical wrangling**. They shape the kind of creative experience and community we want Astrid to make possible.

**[Gaps](README.md)** record what the present system does not yet demonstrate relative to *either* the pillars or these principles. **[Plans](../projects)** target those gaps; evidence updates our understanding. A completed plan is not an automatically closed gap.

## Build Astrid with us

You and your agents can contribute to Astrid's tools, knowledge and creative community. We want people who meaningfully advance the mission to share in ownership of the company behind it, though the legal and allocation model still needs to be worked out.
