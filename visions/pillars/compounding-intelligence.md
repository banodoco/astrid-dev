# Pillar 3 — Intelligence That Compounds

**Every creative agent should benefit from what it and others have learned—and help make that knowledge more useful over time.**

Agents consult relevant local and permitted community experience, apply it in context, observe outcomes, and retain honest, evidence-backed lessons. Useful discoveries can flow back with the user's approval. Public and private knowledge datasets keep their own identity, authority and permissions.

The [Hivemind protocol proposal](../references/compounding-intelligence/hivemind-protocol-proposal.md) explores interoperable knowledge records; [Banodoco Hivemind](https://github.com/banodoco/hivemind) is a proving ground, **not** the entire vision or a claim that federation is already implemented.
