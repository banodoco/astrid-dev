# CA-002 — A meaningful contributor-ownership model

**Applies to:** [Shared Creative Endeavour](../pillars/shared-endeavour.md) · [In the creator's control](../principles.md#in-the-creators-control) · **Assessment:** Open; vision-level commitment, legal and operating terms not established.

## Desired state

The people who meaningfully advance the collective have a genuine, understandable stake in the company behind it. The stated ambition is **100% ownership for contributors advancing the mission**, not merely participation rewards.

## Current state

The ownership intention has been stated by the founder. No eligibility, allocation, vesting, governance, financing or legal instrument has been established within the materials reviewed for this vision.

## Gap

A transparent and legally workable mechanism for translating meaningful contributions—creative, technical, mentoring or otherwise—into ownership and rights, including treatment of existing and future financing, has not yet been specified or implemented.

## Closure evidence

An explicit owner decision and qualified legal/tax review of a concrete model covering eligible contributions, grants/allocations, governance, vesting, changes over time, financing and participant protections; then evidence of its authorized implementation. A declaration alone does not close this gap.

## Sources and reassessment

[Collective Ambition principle](../pillars/shared-endeavour.md). This is **not** an equity offer, promise of automatic entitlement or claim that ownership has been legally transferred.
