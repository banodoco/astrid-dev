# AA-001 — Sustained, ambitious creative work

**Applies to:** [A Tool for Artistic Ambition](../pillars/artistic-ambition.md) · [In the creator's control](../principles.md#in-the-creators-control) · **Assessment:** Open, initial source assessment (2026-11-06); not a live product qualification.

## Desired state

A person can pursue a substantial, distinctive creative vision from early exploration through deep iteration to finished work, retaining control of their artistic decisions and improving their craft.

## Current state

Astrid provides a video editor, generation capabilities, agent-led timeline editing and creative packs. These are substantial foundations, but a coherent, demanding creator-led journey has **not been demonstrated by the evidence reviewed for this vision**.

## Gap

The ceiling on sustained artistic control, continuity, revision and creative quality is not yet established through an ambitious end-to-end project.

## Closure evidence

A real artist-led project is completed through repeated revision, with demonstrable continuity, editable results, preserved creative intent and serious creator/peer assessment. Record what the tools enabled, where they constrained ambition and what remains unresolved.

[Artistic ambition reference principle](../pillars/artistic-ambition.md) · [Existing project plans](../../plans). This assessment must be refreshed against actual completed work and creator evidence; implementation-plan completion alone cannot close it.
