# CA-001 — A culture that challenges people artistically

**Principle:** [Collective Ambition, Shared Ownership](../principle.md) · **Assessment:** Open; founder-described community practices, no current program/outcome audit.

## Desired state

People have compelling reasons to attempt work beyond their current abilities: artistic challenges, collaboration, critiques, showcases and high standards that encourage originality and growth.

## Current state

The founder describes Banodoco community activity and art competitions. This is evidence of an intention and starting point, **not** evidence that a repeatable program demonstrably raises creative ambition or artistic quality.

## Gap

Challenge formats, high-quality review, recognition and reliable feedback loops must become repeatable practices judged by their creative effects rather than participation metrics alone.

## Closure evidence

Run a bounded challenge with clear artistic intent and fair participation; document artists' goals, feedback, completed work and whether it enabled more ambitious efforts. Incorporate genuine creator assessment, including negative findings and barriers.

## Sources and reassessment

[Collective Ambition principle](../principle.md). Any community program needs an accountable owner, accessibility considerations and its own execution/assessment plan. No such plan is authorized by this gap.
