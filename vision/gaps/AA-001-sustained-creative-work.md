# AA-001 — Astrid hasn't yet proven it can sustain ambitious work end to end

**Aspiration:** [Finished artistic work](../pillars/artistic-ambition.md#finished-artistic-work) (primary) · [Approachable, reversible choices](../principles/in-the-creators-control.md#approachable-reversible-choices) (supporting) · **Assessment:** Open, initial source assessment (2026-11-06); not a live product qualification.

## Desired state

A person can pursue a substantial, distinctive creative vision from early exploration through deep iteration to finished work, retaining control of their artistic decisions and improving their craft.

## Current state

Astrid provides a video editor, generation capabilities, agent-led timeline editing and creative packs. These are substantial foundations, but **we haven't yet demonstrated that Astrid can sustain a demanding, creator-led artistic project from exploration through deep revisions to a finished work**. The limits of artistic control, continuity and quality over a long project remain unproven.

## Gap

The ceiling on sustained artistic control, continuity, revision and creative quality is not yet established through an ambitious end-to-end project.

## Closure evidence

A real artist-led project is completed through repeated revision, with demonstrable continuity, editable results, preserved creative intent and serious creator/peer assessment. Record what the tools enabled, where they constrained ambition and what remains unresolved.
