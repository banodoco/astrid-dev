# CA-002 — No implemented contributor-ownership model

**Aspiration:** [A meaningful stake in the collective](../pillars/shared-endeavour.md#a-meaningful-stake-in-the-collective) (primary) · **Assessment:** Open; earlier Banodoco arrangement lapsed, Astrid model and legal terms not established.

## Desired state

The people who meaningfully advance the collective have a genuine, understandable stake in the company behind it. The stated ambition is **100% ownership for contributors advancing the mission**, not merely participation rewards.

## Current state

Banodoco published a contributor-ownership approach, monthly grant lists and reported transfers. Its earlier proposal described splitting 1% per month over 8.5 years, with changing contributor categories and an explicitly provisional legal structure. The program has not been actively maintained during Astrid's recent development. **The historical publications are evidence of earlier commitments, not proof of legally issued equity.** An Astrid-specific arrangement must reconcile those records and existing promises before deciding new eligibility, allocation, governance, financing or implementation.

## Gap

A transparent and legally workable mechanism for translating meaningful contributions—creative, technical, mentoring or otherwise—into ownership and rights, including treatment of existing and future financing, has not yet been specified or implemented.

## Closure evidence

An explicit owner decision and qualified legal/tax review of a concrete model covering eligible contributions, grants/allocations, governance, vesting, changes over time, financing and participant protections; then evidence of its authorized implementation. A declaration alone does not close this gap.
