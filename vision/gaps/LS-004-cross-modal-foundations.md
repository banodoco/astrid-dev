# LS-004 — A creative ecosystem across modalities isn't yet established

**Applies to:** [Agent-Powered Liquid Software](../pillars/liquid-software.md) (primary) · [A Tool for Artistic Ambition](../pillars/artistic-ambition.md) (supporting) · [Open by design](../principles/open-by-design.md) (supporting) · **Assessment:** Open; vision and draft-plan assessment (2026-11-07), not a qualification of current product coverage.

## Desired state

Astrid grows into an open ecosystem of powerful, agent-native, deeply extensible creative foundations across video, images, music, 3D and further modalities. The collective and independent contributors can create, publish, adapt and maintain these foundations; other people and agents can build additional tools and capabilities on top of them, combine them and make ambitious work without forking Astrid's core. Video is a deliberate starting point, not a limit on the underlying philosophy.

## Current state

Astrid is primarily a video editor and timeline, with generation, packs, agent protocols and other supporting capabilities. Video is an exciting medium to establish a strong foundation, but the existing product and its interfaces remain video-centered. [Packs](../../plans/06-packs/plan.md), [VibeComfy extensibility](../../plans/17-vibecomfy-extensibility/plan.md), [timeline foundations](../../plans/18-timeline-editing/plan.md), [Three.js extraction](../../plans/11-threejs-pack/plan.md) and [liquid workspaces](../../plans/24-liquid-workspace/plan.md) are related implementation directions, not evidence of a flourishing cross-modal ecosystem.

## Gap

It is not yet demonstrated that people can establish and share **reusable modality-level foundations** beyond video, or that independent contributors can build on those foundations to create further creative tools. This is broader than [LS-003](LS-003-full-stack-contributions.md), which concerns composable frontend/backend extension contracts within a working host; it is also distinct from proving one artist's end-to-end workflow under [AA-001](../gaps/AA-001-sustained-creative-work.md).

## Closure evidence

On documented supported setups, creators and agents successfully make real work using video plus at least two non-video modality foundations, with preserved files and project control. At least one non-video foundation is contributed outside the core team, and another independent contributor can build and publish a useful additional capability on top of it without a bespoke core fork. Show actual creative results; discoverability, setup, shared agent/user interfaces, extension and update paths, permissions, recovery and meaningful negative cases; and feedback from creators who use these tools. This demonstrates a reusable cross-modal ecosystem, **not** a claim that every imaginable modality has been completed.
