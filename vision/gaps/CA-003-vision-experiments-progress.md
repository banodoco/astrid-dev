# CA-003 — Our vision, experiments and execution aren't yet clearly connected

**Applies to:** [Completely open by design](../principles/open-by-design.md) (primary) · [A Shared Creative Endeavor](../pillars/shared-endeavour.md) (supporting) · **Assessment:** Partially evidenced (2026-11-09; public vision, gaps and project summaries exist, but their relationship to experiments and execution has not been demonstrated end to end).

## Desired state

Someone outside the core team can understand what Astrid is trying to achieve, why particular experiments or projects were undertaken, what was actually tried, what the evidence showed, what decisions changed, and which gaps remain. This trail should be accessible, candid about failed or abandoned approaches, and kept current as work develops.

## Current state

The [public Vision page](https://astrid.haus/vision) and [canonical Vision index](../README.md) now publish pillars, principles, assessed gaps, and a project catalog. Those are meaningful pieces of transparency, but they do not yet demonstrate a consistently maintained, readable chain from stated ambition through concrete experiments and execution to outcomes and changed plans. Existing public documents are a start, not proof that someone can reliably follow that reasoning.

## Gap

We have not communicated our vision, the experiments exploring it, actual execution, and the gaps between intention and reality well enough as one connected story. Unlike [CA-001](CA-001-creative-challenge-culture.md), this is about public understanding and accountability, not establishing challenges or artistic participation; unlike [CA-002](CA-002-contributor-ownership.md), it is not about legally implementing contributor ownership.

## Closure evidence

Publish a maintained public trail for at least three substantive decisions or experiments, each connecting the relevant vision and gap to the hypothesis, work attempted, evidence, outcome, resulting decision, and what remains unresolved.

Include at least one negative, abandoned, or changed approach without presenting it as success; verify the source links and revisions. Have independent contributors unfamiliar with those efforts locate the rationale and current outcome, record where they were confused, and demonstrate that resulting improvements make the chain understandable. Reassess after subsequent substantive work rather than only at the initial publication.

## Sources and reassessment

[Vision and gaps](../README.md) · [Public Vision page](https://astrid.haus/vision) · [project catalog](../../plans/index.md) · [documentation contribution workflow](../../docs/contribution-workflow.md).
