# CI-002 — Independent Hiveminds don't yet work together

**Applies to:** [Intelligence That Compounds](../pillars/compounding-intelligence.md) (primary) · [Open by design](../principles/open-by-design.md) (supporting) · [In the creator's control](../principles/in-the-creators-control.md) (supporting) · **Assessment:** Open, proposal compared with source (2026-11-06).

## Desired state

Independent personal, team and community knowledge datasets can preserve attribution, conditions, evidence and owner-controlled access while exchanging permitted references and learnings without merging into one central authority.

## Current state

[Banodoco Hivemind](../../plans/22-hivemind-protocol/inputs.md) documents source-custodied messages, revisioned resources, an accepted search head and editorial contribution operations. The [uploaded October 29 protocol proposal](../../plans/22-hivemind-protocol/protocol-proposal.md) proposes dataset-scoped entries, typed links and `find`/`inspect`/`contribute`. **These are not yet identical proven contracts.**

## Gap

Versioned interoperable record/identity/reference and permission semantics, a local/private dataset implementation and one real cross-dataset boundary need a reconciled specification and evidence.

## Closure evidence

Two separately owned datasets can exchange a scoped, independently attributed reference or approved sanitized result, preserving revision identity, provenance and access decisions; denied, stale and revoked cases do not leak source content. Banodoco behavior is preserved or safely, explicitly migrated with evidence.
