# In the creator's control

## Statement

Creators should have final say over every part of their creative environments.

## Explanation

Astrid should make it possible to choose features and services, control agent permissions, and decide what stays private or becomes shared. Even core workings should be adaptable, without requiring artists to become experts in the underlying technology.

## Characteristics

### Control at every layer

Creators should be able to change the capabilities they rely on.

They can choose, inspect, replace, or extend features and services, including fundamental parts of the environment, while keeping the work and its history understandable.

### Explicit permissions and privacy

Agents should act within boundaries the creator can understand and change.

Access to files, networks, services, and spending must be scoped and inspectable. Creators should be able to deny or revoke permissions and decide what information is retained or shared.

### Approachable, reversible choices

Control should be easy to exercise without technical expertise.

Interfaces should show the meaningful consequences of decisions, support correction and recovery, and make it possible to undo or refuse risky changes rather than treating control as an advanced setting.
